# Finding: How do agents recover from failed steps?

## Question
How do agents recover from failed steps?

## Findings
A workshop study reports that agents which detect a tool failure and
replan from the failed step complete more tasks than agents that stop at
the failure (tier-3). The evidence rests on that one workshop study.

- [Recovering from tool failures](https://openalex.org/W3003) — tier-3 — Workshop on Agent Failures
