# PP-891 Baseline 2: Two Works Cited by DOI

## Subject

What the works identified by two DOIs contribute, and what evidence each
offers for its claims.

## Motivation

This subject is the second baseline run for PP-891. It records how the web
profile cites and tiers sources given as DOI resolver URLs, before any change
to the profile or the CLI.

## Scope

In scope: the two works below. Sources to cite, each by the URL given here:

- https://doi.org/10.1145/371920.372066
- https://doi.org/10.1145/3448016.3457556

## Questions

- What does each work contribute, and what evidence does it offer?

## Success Criteria

Both URLs above appear as cited sources in the findings, each with a venue
and a tier.
